Core Design Philosophy & Principles

Four unbreakable tenets governing every user interface decision across the national and regional donor registry pipeline.

bloodtype
Principle 01

Life-Critical Clarity

Zero ambiguous iconography. High-contrast typography for blood group classifications (A+, O-, Platelets), hemoglobin measures, and cross-matching alerts. Immediate cognitive recognition under harsh clinic lighting.

Rule: No text below 12px check_circle
spa
Principle 02

Reassurance & Calm

Clinical white space and disciplined rounded containers (8px–12px). Elimination of high-anxiety medical graphics or dramatic red fills that elevate donor heart rates during pre-donation vitals screening.

Rule: Soft tint fills for notices check_circle
touch_app
Principle 03

Emergency Speed

Single-action appointment dispatching, large touch targets (>=48px) for mobile camp kiosks, and zero multifold menus for emergency bed allocations during sudden trauma surges.

Rule: Triage flow ≤ 3 clicks check_circle
accessibility_new
Principle 04

Universal Accessibility

Strict WCAG AAA for vital status metrics, color-blind safe badges equipped with both alphanumeric strings and symbolic glyphs, accompanied by full screen-reader live region support.

Rule: Color is never sole signifier check_circle

rule Section 06

Form Design & Donor Flow Rules

Clinical interaction principles for pre-donation medical history questionnaires and eligibility assessments.

check_circle DO: Chunk Questions & Explain Intent

Present medical screening items as singular, focused questions with plain-language rationales and binary (Yes/No) large touch toggles.

Have you received any tattoos in the last 3 months? Why we ask: Protects blood recipients against blood-borne pathogens during incubation periods.
cancel DON'T: Dense Medical Jargon Scrolls

Never dump a 40-item dense text questionnaire with unexplained clinical terminology (e.g. "variant Creutzfeldt-Jakob etiology") without human translation.

ANTI-PATTERN: Unreadable Dense Checkboxes
[ ] Patient exhibits subacute spongiform encephalopathy risk factors under clause 4.8.2.3...
[ ] Has ingested acetylsalicylic acid compounds within 48hr window prior to apheresis...
check_circle DO: Constructive Deferral Messages

Provide empathetic, health-positive deferral communications that guide the donor toward self-care and clearly state their exact return date.

favorite
Take care of your health first! Your hemoglobin was 11.8 g/dL today (minimum is 12.5). You can safely re-test on Nov 12, 2025. Consider iron-rich nutrition in the meantime.
cancel DON'T: Punitive Rejection Modals

Never display alarming bright red modal dialogs with stigmatizing wording like "DISQUALIFIED", "FAILED", or "REJECTED".

error
APPLICATION REJECTED Failed medical standards check. Donor disqualified from further blood banking operations.
